--------------------------------------------------------

- advapi32   (Win32 Registry HKLM CurrentVersion + GetUserNameW)

Note: Zero third-party dependencies. Uses only C++20 STL, openscarlett_core
(pe_inspector / DllRoutingTable / IsWindowsKnownDll), and native Win32 APIs.
Includes explicit <shellapi.h> and <winver.h> after <windows.h> so builds
remain clean under WIN32_LEAN_AND_MEAN across MSVC, Clang-CL, and MinGW-w64.
