=== SUMMARY OF DELIVERABLES ===
1. Single-Wave Lockstep LDS Barrier Insertion:
   - ParseXboxShaderContainer() now always honors non-zero PSV0 compute threadgroup dimensions
     (NumThreadsX/Y/Z), overriding XS2 header defaults so [numthreads(x,y,z)] and SPIR-V
     OpExecutionMode LocalSize match the PSV0 contract.
   - Added InsertSingleWaveLockstepLdsBarriers() pass in OptimizeRdna2Ir() for compute/mesh
     shaders with 1 < (tg_x * tg_y * tg_z) <= 64 (single AMD Wave64 group that splits into 2
     independent Wave32 warps on NVIDIA/Intel GPUs).
   - Performs intra-block and cross-block CFG LDS hazard analysis (write/atomic -> read/atomic,
     read -> write/atomic) without an intervening explicit IrOp::Barrier, inserting explicit
     workgroup barriers (HLSL GroupMemoryBarrierWithGroupSync() + SPIR-V OpControlBarrier
     Execution=Workgroup(2), Memory=Workgroup(2), Semantics=AcquireRelease|WorkgroupMemory(0x108)).
   - Records telemetry in Rdna2IrOptStats::inserted_lockstep_lds_barriers,
     Rdna2IrModule::inserted_lockstep_lds_barriers, and Rdna2LiftResult::inserted_lockstep_lds_barriers.
   - Verified zero spurious insertions when explicit s_barrier exists, when only LDS writes occur,
     or for multi-wave (>64 thread) threadgroups.

2. 5 Explicit No-Op Opcodes (AMD RDNA2 / GFX10.3):
   - s_icache_inv      (SOPP 0x13, 0xBF930000)
   - s_ttracedata      (SOPP 0x16, 0xBF960000)
   - s_code_end        (SOPP 0x1F, 0xBF9F0000) — including unreachable trailing s_code_end padding after s_endpgm
   - s_ttracedata_imm  (SOPP 0x28, 0xBFA8xxxx)
   - s_version         (SOPK 0x01, 0xB080xxxx)
   - Cleanly encoded, decoded, disassembled, lowered to IR no-ops, interpreted on CPU, and
     lifted to valid HLSL/DXIL (SM 6.5 / SM 6.2) + SPIR-V (validated by Khronos spirv-val).

3. Missing ISA Paths Added for Differential Verification:
   - s_bfe_u32 (SOP2 0x27) & s_bfe_i32 (SOP2 0x28): full 5-bit offset (src1[4:0]) & 7-bit width
     (src1[22:16]) decode, IR lowering, constant-folding, CPU interpreter, and UB-free HLSL + SPIR-V lifting.
   - v_cvt_pknorm_i16_f32 (VOP3 0x368), v_cvt_pknorm_u16_f32 (VOP3 0x369),
     v_cvt_norm_i16_f16 (VOP1 0x63 / VOP3 0x1E3), v_cvt_norm_u16_f16 (VOP1 0x64 / VOP3 0x1E4):
     full decode, IR, constant-folding, CPU interpreter, and HLSL + SPIR-V lifting (NaN->0, clamp, RNE).
   - Rdna2TextureFormat::Uint4 contract in StageContractTable + Rdna2IrModule + HLSL Texture2D<uint4> +
     SPIR-V OpTypeImage(..., u32, ...) + Rdna2CpuTexture::AllocateR32u(mip_levels) / LoadR32uRgba()
     matching D3D12 / RDNA2 T# missing-component fill (R=0 on OOB, G=0, B=0, A=1) bit-exact on both
     NVIDIA GTX 1650 Ti and Microsoft D3D12 WARP.
