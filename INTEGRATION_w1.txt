OpenScarlett Worker 1 Integration Notes (Sprint 9 + Sprint 10: d3d12_xs COM Proxies & Multi-Queue)
====================================================================================================

   (Header added alongside it: `src/gpu/d3d12_xs/proxies_x.h`)

   ```cmake

   target_link_libraries(test_d3d12_xs_proxies_multiqueue PRIVATE d3d12_xs d3d12 dxgi d3dcompiler)
   ```

3. Integration hooks exposed for Worker 2 (`TitleVAMirror` & `DescriptorSgprBridge`):
   - `DeviceX::SetGuestGpuVaTranslatorHook(GuestGpuVaTranslatorFn fn, void* user_ctx)`
   - `DeviceX::SetDescriptorCreateHook(DescriptorCreateHookFn fn, void* user_ctx)`
   - `CommandQueueX::SetSubmitHook(QueueSubmitHookFn fn, void* user_ctx)`
   All hooks use lock-free atomic loads (zero overhead when unset) and receive unwrapped host pointers.

4. Summary of implemented scope:
   - All 10 Xbox-ABI COM proxies in `src/gpu/d3d12_xs/proxies_x.{h,cpp}` (`ResourceXProxy`, `HeapXProxy`,
     `DescriptorHeapXProxy`, `PipelineStateXProxy`, `RootSignatureXProxy`, `FenceXProxy`,
     `CommandAllocatorXProxy`, `QueryHeapXProxy`, `CommandSignatureXProxy`, `StateObjectXProxy` +
     `ID3D12StateObjectProperties`) with 64-shard lock-free-read `Unwrap*` helpers, `WIDL_EXPLICIT_AGGREGATE_RETURNS`
     MinGW-w64 sret ABI parity, and portable `OS_D3D12_IID(T)` macro (MSVC / Clang-CL / MinGW-w64).
   - Xbox D3D12 extensions: `CommandListX::WriteBufferImmediate`, `SetViewInstanceMask`, `OMSetDepthBounds`,
     `SetSamplePositions`, and `DeviceX::GetCopyableFootprints` host-refusal parity.
   - Thread-safe multi-threaded command recording + Direct/Async Compute/Copy multi-queue `FenceX` synchronization.
